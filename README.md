# Black Tap Bar Book

Phone web app for the Black Tap Soho bar, floor and kitchen team: every drink spec with step-by-step picture guides, allergens, spec practice, opening and closing checklists for the bar, floor and kitchen, the 86 list, the shift report, and My pay: each person logs the shifts they work and gets an estimate of their pay, tronc, tax, National Insurance, student loan and pension by day, week, month or year, kept on their own phone only.

Team logins add a live shift board shared by everyone on shift. The bell opens the Team page: For you (what's new and what's waiting on you, each a shortcut to where it's dealt with), Tonight at a glance, then a card for each part with what's waiting in it, each part on a page of its own. Team logins also add tonight's football on the screens (added by managers), each drink's house photo and how-to video link (added by managers), the last handover on the opening page, live checklists (each item Not started, In progress, Done, Blocked or Not needed tonight, with who, when and why, and each list started and finished; managers change any list in the app, and every version is kept), date labels for what each side opens (juices, sauces, cakes: who opened, changed or binned each one and when, with what's due tomorrow, today or overdue on the Shift pages, the dashboard, Live and the closing report), the rota a week at a time (drafted, then published, with open shifts, offers, hands up and cover), a calendar in Rota (the next 7 days or a month at a time: bank holidays in England and Wales, faith days, big games, London events, the nights that change how busy a bar is, the games on the screens, school holidays, half terms, events and closed days added by managers, and each person's shifts and time off, with time off asked for from a day), restocking from the cellar (asked for, given to someone, brought up and confirmed), the 86 board, issues and handover, a list of what's still to do before sign-off, shift reports with photos and sign-off (every report and incident in Reports, night by night, older ones a page at a time), broadcasts to everyone, the bar, the floor or the kitchen (with who has read them), time off and swaps, incidents, Report a concern (with photos, to the team or managers only, and anonymous if you like), training sign-off drink by drink, team chat (the whole team, private chats and groups, with photos and @ pings, shown while the app is open), an Activity timeline of who did what (staff see their own side: bar, floor or kitchen), and, for managers, Live (tonight as it happens: what's blocked, overdue or waiting), Activity with filters and each item's history, closing KPIs night by night and by person, WhatsApp, Email and the phone's own share sheet, and spreadsheet downloads of each section's full history, the audit trail included. They are switched on. The Features page under Bar info lists every feature with what it's for and what staff and managers can do with it, has a tour that opens each one in turn, and lets the owner switch features off for the venue. How they were set up, what keeps them secure and how to recover are in [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).

Temperatures, line cleans, cleaning checks and accidents are logged in Alert65, and waste, stock counts and ordering in Nory, so Bar Book doesn't do them. What it saved before 25 October 2026 is read-only and in the managers' downloads.

Open it at https://rickdsage.github.io/bar-book/ and add it to your home screen (Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen). Always use the venue's allergen matrix as the final word.

## Setting up a laptop

1. Node.js 22 and Java 21 (the Firebase emulators need Java).
2. `npm ci`
3. `npx playwright install chromium` for the browser tests.
4. The drinks data lives in the Black Tap Drinks project folder, not in this repo. The build reads it from `DATA_DIR` (default `/mnt/project-files/black-tap-menu/data/`); set `DATA_DIR=/path/to/data/` to build somewhere else. The rules tests don't need it.

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Business day:** 6am to 6am UK time, so a close that ends at 2am belongs to the night before. Checklists, the Tonight counts and report grouping all use it, and times always show in UK time whatever a phone is set to.
- **Saving:** with no signal, changes wait on the phone and a banner says how many. Nothing is called sent until the server has it, and refused changes say so.
- **One record per task, with a version:** each change raises the version and writes its event (the audit trail) in the same write. Two phones changing the same item can't overwrite each other: the one working from an old copy is refused and told, and a change that syncs late can't add a second event.
- **Taking things back:** only whoever did an item, or a manager, takes it back, and after a few minutes it needs a reason. Blocked and Not needed tonight need a reason; only managers mark an item not needed, and it doesn't count towards what's required.
- **Lists can be changed in the app:** a manager rewords, adds, moves or takes off items and picks the days one shows, and the new version goes on every phone in place of the standard one. Each item keeps its id, so tonight's ticks stay with it when the list changes. Every version is kept with who saved it and when, and any of them, or the standard list, can be put back. Two managers saving at once can't overwrite each other without being told. The kitchen's lists start from the Food Standards Agency's Safer Food, Better Business opening and closing checks, for the kitchen to make its own.
- **Rota:** managers build a week as a draft only they can see, then publish it. They build it on a grid with a row for each person and a column for each day: they pick the times and the side, then tap a day in someone's row to put them on, and tap a draft again to take it off. Open shifts, offers, notes and several days at once go through the form under the grid. Once the week is published, every change is saved with who made it and when, in the shift's own history, in Activity and in the spreadsheets. A week can be copied from the week before (anyone who has left becomes an open shift). A shift with nobody on it is an open shift, and can be offered to one person, who takes it or turns it down. On an open shift, or one whose person can't make it, anyone can put their hand up and a manager decides; only managers put someone on a shift. Anyone who can't make their own shift says why, and it stays theirs until a manager gives it to someone else. Hours run from start to finish, before breaks. Managers are warned, but not stopped, before putting someone on a shift that clashes with another or with time off they have asked for or been given.
- **Date labels:** each side keeps a list of what it dates once it's opened, with how many days each keeps. Opening one dates it today and gives the use-by date to write on it (the pack's own date can bring it forward, never later). Changed it means the old one was binned and a new one opened and labelled, and Binned means used up or thrown away with no new one opened. Each tap keeps who and when, and the change before, so a label changed after its use-by shows on the record. Only managers add, change or take off items; the days are filled in only where Bar Book's lists already had them (desserts 2, strawberry and raspberry 14, maraschino cherries 28), and the rest are for the venue to set.
- **Lists are procedures:** started, then finished. Finishing with items left needs a manager and a reason, and a finished list is locked until a manager reopens it with a reason.
- **Restocking keeps asked, brought up and received apart**, so asking is never taken as proof something arrived. No stock level changes in Bar Book; stock is counted in Nory.
- **Overdue:** in progress for over 30 minutes, or a restock waiting over 15 minutes for someone to take it on or to confirm it arrived. Each is shown with words, never colour alone.
- **Calendar:** the next 7 days or a month at a time in Rota on the Team page, from three months back to two years ahead. The next 7 days lists each day with what's on, the games on the screens and your shifts; tapping one opens it in the month. Bank holidays for England and Wales are built in from gov.uk up to the end of 2028 (`BANK_HOLS` in `src/template.html`: add the next years when gov.uk publishes them). So are the days that fall on a rule each year (`notable()`: Burns Night, Pancake Day, Valentine's Day, St David's, St Patrick's, St George's and St Andrew's Days, Mothering Sunday, Easter, Father's Day, Notting Hill Carnival, Halloween, Bonfire Night, the Lord Mayor's Show, Remembrance Sunday, Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve and the nights the clocks change, on the Saturday since they change in the early hours of Sunday) and, to the end of 2028, the days organisers or the moon set (`ON_DAYS`, with its sources: faith days such as Diwali, Eid, Hanukkah and Chinese New Year, England's Six Nations games and the big finals with their channels, the London Marathon, Wimbledon and Winter Wonderland). Comic cons, Pride and the like aren't in until their organisers publish the dates: managers add them as events. Faith days that follow the moon say "expected", as they can move by a day. School holidays and half terms differ by borough, so none are built in: managers add the ones that matter to the team, and events and days the venue is shut, and change or remove them. Tapping a day shows what's on it, your shifts, your time off (managers see everyone's) and the games on the screens; from there anyone asks for time off, as from Tell the managers, warned about their shifts in those days.
- **Manager close and handover:** a part of the Team page for managers. Each night the closing manager answers twelve questions (every department closed, sales report, cash and card, voids and discounts, service charge and tips, breaks, incidents, complaints, food safety, equipment, security, tomorrow covered), each yes or no, some n/a. A no opens what needs saying about it (how much over or short and on which till, what equipment and whether it's safe to use, and so on), and the handover won't send until it's filled in. Under the questions is what Bar Book saw tonight in every department: reports not sent, blocked, overdue or skipped checklist items, late restocks, overdue labels, urgent issues and open concerns, counted from the live data and saved with the handover, so they can't be ticked away. A no on food safety, equipment or security raises an urgent issue in the same write, which the whole team sees until someone fixes it; a no on cash stays with the managers and the owner. There's one handover a night, and once sent it can't be changed. The next manager reads it and accepts it (For you nags until they do), saying what they'll do about any risks. The owner sees anything marked for them under For you. Staff can't read handovers. Managers can add up to ten of the venue's own questions (a yes means all's well, and each can be marked so a no is a risk that raises an urgent issue like security does); a no on one asks what happened and who has been told. It can be switched off for the venue.
- **Pre-service briefing:** a part of the Team page for everyone, first in the list. A manager posts tonight's briefing before service: covers, bookings and large parties, specials, allergies and dietary notes, sections, breaks and tonight's focus, with a button that brings in last night's handover note. What's 86'd now is shown with it from the 86 board. Staff see it under For you until they've read it, and managers see who has and who hasn't. Changing it asks everyone to read it again. Staff can't post one. It can be switched off for the venue.
- **Guest recovery:** a part of the Team page. Anyone logs a guest problem (wrong order, slow, cold, missing item, allergy and so on) with the table, what went wrong, what the guest wants, what was done and any comp given, up to the limit the owner sets for staff and for managers (in pounds, kept as pence). Escalated ones go to managers under For you, and a manager closes each with the resolution and any follow-up. The same table or kind of problem twice in a night is pointed out. Comp limits are what a person may give away, not menu prices. It can be switched off for the venue.
- **Features can be switched off for the venue:** the owner switches off any of allergens, Practise, My pay, the briefing, football, guest recovery, chat, the rota, the calendar, time off and swaps, incidents, the manager close and handover, the KPIs, training and manager feedback for everyone, for example the rota and time off where they're done in Nory. A switched-off feature is hidden with its tiles and pop-ups, and nothing saved under it is deleted, so switching it back on brings it all back. With the rota off, its tab becomes Calendar; with the calendar off too, managers keep it as Requests, for running late and, while they're on, time off and swaps. Each phone remembers the last list it saw, so a switched-off tab stays hidden while it connects and when nobody is logged in.
- **Start fresh (the owner):** in Team logins on the Team page. It clears the shift work for everyone once the venue has finished trying the app out (the rota, asks, reports with their photos and reviews, the checklists and their history, restocking, the 86 board and log, issues, concerns, broadcasts and the old board and stock requests) and keeps the logins, the audit trail, lists, date labels, drink media, the calendar, games, incidents, training, feedback, chats and the old records. The owner downloads what to keep, sees how many of each it would clear, then types their PIN and START FRESH. The rules allow it only within 15 minutes of signing in, and only after that sign-in has recorded what it found in `resets`, so the app signs in afresh with the PIN and records it before clearing anything. Every phone drops its copies when it hears of one. See [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).
- **History is locked:** sent reports, the 86 log, decisions, closed incidents, fixed issues and account changes can't be edited. The old waste, temperature and stock count records can't be added to, edited or deleted.

## Known limitations

- Nory also does rotas. If the venue runs the rota in Nory, keep one of the two as the one the team works from, or shifts will disagree.
- No notifications on phones where the app is closed (date labels due tomorrow and a published rota included), and no automatic emails: both need Cloud Functions, which need the paid Blaze plan. Updates arrive while the app is open, with pop-ups and badges. The WhatsApp and Email buttons open WhatsApp or the phone's email app with the text written in, and the person picks who it goes to.
- Done and required on a finished list are counted by the phone that taps Finish. The rules can't count the tasks, so the tasks and their events are the record of what was done.
- The audit trail can't be edited or deleted from the app, by anyone, the owner included. It isn't tamper-proof: anyone with access to the Firebase console can change any record, so keep console access to the project owners.
- The KPIs count taps, not work. Use them to ask questions, and tell the team that their changes are logged with their name and the time, and why (the ICO's guidance on monitoring workers is linked on the KPIs page).
- One venue per Firebase project.
- Photos are stored in the database and cleared after 30 days, because Firebase Storage also needs Blaze.
- PINs are 6 to 12 digits for everyone. Firebase won't accept a password shorter than 6, so 4-digit PINs aren't possible.
- Building needs the drinks data folder.
